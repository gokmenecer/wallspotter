What we collect

WallSpotter does not have user accounts, comments or sign-up forms. If you send us a message through the contact form, the name, email address and message you enter are delivered to us by the form service Web3Forms and used only to reply to you.

Cookies

WallSpotter does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across websites.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It does not use cookies, does not store personal data and does not follow you across other websites. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Embedded content from other websites

Wallpaper images on this site are loaded from Pinterest (i.pinimg.com). When your browser loads these images, Pinterest receives standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type, in the same way as if you had visited Pinterest directly.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
